About Me

Go big, or go home.
I like to understand people's thoughts and motivatives when they make decisions. Bringing these observations and understandings into interface design makes every element in the interface have its own meaning. I also hope to help users solve their problems through a simple, clean, and hierarchical interface.

Besides UIUX designer, I was also a front-end engineer, and my familiarity with website languages allowed me to save a lot of time when communicating with engineers.

At outsourcing company, in addition to basic interface design, I have participated in many early-stage interviews and plannings of projects, and have a good understanding of the collection and organization of requirements. Due to the features diversity, I have completed many briefings of proposals with the team and accumulated a lot of experience in public briefing and product optimization.

At product company, I understood that it is the responsibility of a product designer to find the most appropriate balance among company benefits, project goals, and design quality. I also realized that in addition to the precision and efficiency of execution, offering reliability and stability might be the value of senior designer.

While I am not doing design, I am also a fitness lover who go to the gym three or four times a week. I also like to watch Netflix or listen to podcasts in my spare time.
