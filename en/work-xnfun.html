UI/UX Design

XnFun Storage Server App

Overview
XnFun App is an exclusive app for XnBay Smart Storage Server(Private Cloud), XnBay focuses on free monthly fee, absolutely private storage space with huge capacity, and you can easily transfer and manage files through XnFun App.

At the beginning, the client already had the completed XnFun App, but the UI design need to be improved, so they hope we can help redesign the visual. After couple weeks, the client added some new functions to let us design the UX together, such as the music player function.
  • Role
    UI/UX Designer
  • Duration
    6 month
  • Team
    1 PM, 1 Designer, 2 Developer
Challenge
Because XnFun App must connect to the private cloud first, then you can operate all the function, so it's really difficult to do competitor analysis. You can't just download the app, you need to have the physical storage.
Reskin
Since XnFun is still an app that mainly presents multimedia content, so the strategy of Reskin is to not affect content browsing.

The overall layout is referrd to the simple style like Facebook and Instagram. The primary color follows the blue form logo, and I spent lots of time on the illustration of empty status to improve product recognition, it also can smooth the anxiety while the user operating and waiting.

Final Design

Welcome Page
Use illustrations to add brand colors to enhance product connectivity and add interest to products, so that users who log in for the first time can use the app calmly.
Local File
The concise layout allows users to focus more on their file content.
XnBay Management
Use different layouts to distinguish function types, so that users can quickly identify different functions when browsing files.
Story
美好的回憶可以構成一個動人的故事，XnFun的故事可以分享給家庭成員或朋友，也加入了社群的「按讚、留言、分享」功能，透過互動讓回憶更溫暖。

Use different layouts to distinguish function types, so that users can more quickly identify different functions when browsing files.
My Account
Manage various settings and operation items through My Account, including the most important backup settings, and present each local folder in the form of a card, which is convenient for users to track the backup progress.
Music
Music is a new function, so the UX also needs to be orgamized. The process and interface refer to Spotify and KKBOX, and then integrate into the existing process of XnFun, hoping that users can listen to music smoothly like a general music app.

Learnings

Although the poject started with Reskin, I also had to understand the existing process and structure, and I did a lot of research on file management apps, such as Google Drive. During the project, I also learned more about the backup and file transfer functions.

The newly added music function also gave me the opportunity to go deep into the details of music player, such as the logic of random playback and the effect of the to-be-played list. Overall, it's an interesting design process, and it's one of the few products I've ever touched To C.
